# 荔枝天赋 / Lychee Talent — 1.0.0

## 中文

**找到这一战的天赋，把精力留给战斗。**

荔枝天赋以 WCL 高表现记录为参考，为不同副本与首领整理天赋方案。打开天赋页，找到当前场景，双击即可应用；从日常大米到团本攻坚，更快选到适合这一战的天赋。

- **按场景推荐**：按副本、首领与专精查找推荐天赋，支持英雄／史诗团本筛选，并可查看记录信息与 WCL 来源。
- **到场提醒**：进入对应副本，或到达团本首领附近时，提醒切换荔枝推荐天赋或已关联的个人方案；点击后才应用，战斗中延后提醒。
- **你的方案库**：导入、保存和编辑个人天赋，自选图标，并关联多个副本或首领。
- **动作条随方案走**：选择独立动作条，或使用该专精的默认布局，减少切换后的重复整理。
- **简洁统一**：融入原生天赋页，中英文界面，右上角齿轮统一控制切换提醒。

推荐来自高表现实战记录，实际选择仍取决于版本、装备、队伍与打法。数据随插件更新，在游戏内查看来源即可了解记录背景。

## English

**Find your best build for the encounter. Keep your focus on the fight.**

Lychee Talent brings together talent builds from high-performing WCL records for individual dungeons and bosses. Open your talent window, find the encounter and double-click to apply a build—from everyday Mythic+ runs to raid progression.

- **Encounter-based recommendations:** browse by dungeon, boss and specialization, filter Heroic or Mythic raids, and inspect record details with links to the WCL source.
- **Timely reminders:** on reaching a dungeon or raid boss area, choose a Lychee recommended build or a linked personal build. Talents change only when you click; reminders wait until combat ends.
- **Your own build library:** import, save and edit builds, choose their icons, and link them to multiple dungeons or bosses.
- **Action bars that follow your build:** use an independent layout or the specialization's default layout to reduce repeated setup.
- **A familiar home:** integrated with the native talent window, with Chinese and English text and a reminder switch under the settings gear.

Recommendations offer a starting point drawn from strong real-world performance. Your best choice can vary with the patch, gear, group and strategy. Data ships with addon updates; source details remain available in game.
